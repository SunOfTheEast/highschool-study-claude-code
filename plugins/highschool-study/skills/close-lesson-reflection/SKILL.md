name: close-lesson-reflection
description: Give the student explicit authority over continuing, adjusting, pausing, or closing a Lesson.
user-invocable: false
allowed-tools: Read, Glob, Grep, Edit, Skill, mcp__plugin_highschool-study_study-markdown__trace_search
---

1. Present the Lesson evidence by criterion, including support level, conflicts, and uncertainty. Treat card fields literally: problem category = card_search.goal (graph.goal.primary); method shell = card_search.methods (graph.method). Before changing Plan progress, call `trace_search` once with the current `planId` and `limit: 100`, then use only active Trace and `cardsByPath`; copy every non-empty category cell from `cardsByPath[cardPath].goal.primary` and every method cell from the card's method fields. Never infer or paraphrase a category from Lesson prose, a solution route, a method, or surface wording; a missing card or goal is `unknown` and cannot satisfy a category requirement. Build a temporary matrix: `criterion | lesson/block | cardPath | problem category (graph.goal.primary) | method shell (graph.method) | first exposure | assessment | current support | prior support | qualifies`. A same-card retry is practice, not unseen transfer. The chronology rule is that consecutive means adjacent evidence-bearing attempts; do not skip supported, failed, or conflicting attempts. The category rule remains: method structure is not a problem category. Compare exact non-empty goal values; two different method shells with one goal value still count as one problem category. A correct answer may inform discussion, but missing active Trace blocks attainment. Ask what helped and what did not without making an answer mandatory. An explicit pause or close request is already the student choice: honor it immediately rather than asking the student to choose again.
2. Offer `continue / adjust / pause / close` in the student's language. The student owns the choice. Silence, attainment, delegated discretion, elapsed time, or Task completion is not a close instruction.
3. On continue or adjust, preserve the Lesson as active and revise only future blocks. On pause, record the explicit choice and resumable point. On close, write the Lesson Summary from active source-linked evidence and record closure separately from attainment.
4. Closing a Lesson never closes its Plan. If Plan capability evidence now meets its standard, show it; only an explicit student choice to complete the Plan may route to `highschool-study:consolidate-plan-memory`. Otherwise continue the Plan or prepare the next Lesson.
5. Once the final audit decision is `active`, `complete`, or `replan`, persist it before reporting or routing. Map `complete` to `status: completed`; map `active` and `replan` to `status: active`. Update `## Lesson Index` with actual Lesson states and sources. Update `## Current Position` with met, unmet, and conflicting criteria. Update `## Next Lesson Candidate` with the grounded next candidate or an explicit no-next-lesson result. Update `## Plan Summary` with active evidence, the student's choice, and the audit decision.
6. Reread the Plan after the write and verify frontmatter and all four sections. Only then send the student the Plan decision or route an explicitly chosen completion to consolidation. If the write or reread fails, say the decision is not yet persisted and make no changed-state claim.
