user-invocable: false

1. Read the current Lesson's active Trace, working notes and direct sources. Present what the Lesson established, what remains uncertain, actual support and conflicts. Ask what helped only when useful; an answer is optional.
2. If the student already asked to pause or close, honor that choice without showing another menu. Otherwise offer continue, adjust, pause or close in the student's language. Silence, attainment, elapsed time and Task completion are not closure.
3. On continue or adjust, keep the Lesson active and change only future Blocks. On pause, record the resumable point. On close, write Reflection and Lesson Summary from active source-linked evidence, and record closure separately from attainment.
4. Closing a Lesson never closes its Plan. Return to the Coach route for Plan review, next-Lesson preparation or an explicitly chosen Plan-completion discussion. Plan-wide audit and persistence do not belong to this Lesson closure Skill.
