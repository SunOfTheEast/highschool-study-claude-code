---
name: close-lesson-reflection
description: Give the student explicit authority over continuing, adjusting, pausing, or closing a Lesson.
user-invocable: false
allowed-tools: Read, Glob, Grep, Edit, Skill
---

1. Present the Lesson evidence by criterion, including support level, conflicts, and uncertainty. Before changing Plan progress, build a temporary matrix: `criterion | lesson/block | cardPath | problem category | first exposure | assessment | current support | prior support | qualifies`. A same-card retry is practice, not unseen transfer. The chronology rule is that consecutive means adjacent evidence-bearing attempts; do not skip supported, failed, or conflicting attempts. The category rule is that method structure is not a problem category. A correct answer may inform discussion, but missing active Trace blocks attainment. Ask what helped and what did not without making an answer mandatory. An explicit pause or close request is already the student choice: honor it immediately rather than asking the student to choose again.
2. Offer `continue / adjust / pause / close` in the student's language. The student owns the choice. Silence, attainment, delegated discretion, elapsed time, or Task completion is not a close instruction.
3. On continue or adjust, preserve the Lesson as active and revise only future blocks. On pause, record the explicit choice and resumable point. On close, write the Lesson Summary from active source-linked evidence and record closure separately from attainment.
4. Closing a Lesson never closes its Plan. If Plan capability evidence now meets its standard, show it; only an explicit student choice to complete the Plan may route to `highschool-study:consolidate-plan-memory`. Otherwise continue the Plan or prepare the next Lesson.
