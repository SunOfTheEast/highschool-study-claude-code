# Plan Next Cycle

## Reconstruct the decision

Read the Roadmap, current Plan, relevant child Handoffs, confirmed profiles,
and learning principles. Handoffs are compact entry points; open their Trace,
Block, Card, Session, or lower Claim sources only when the detail could change
the decision. Active Trace owns recorded attempt outcome, actual support, and
method evidence. A score, card method label, Task state, or preparation intent
may locate a question but cannot decide capability.

Reconstruct change over time: independence, support, transfer, retention,
recurring reasoning, and response to teaching. Missing or invalidated sources
make a claim uncertain. If history is broad or conflicting, delegate one
focused retrieval or counter-evidence question to the lesson designer; the
delegate returns sources and conflicts, never a verdict.

Ask the current student one question at a time about how they interpret the
cycle, what matters now, and changed constraints. Before proposing, summarize
historical evidence plus the current account and invite correction. Choose one
leverage point that matters to the Roadmap and can change within one Plan.

## Update the current Plan

The Plan owns `## Lesson Tree`:

- Candidate: a future Lesson possibility with no file or Session;
- Child: one real Lesson link whose status is read from that child file.

After a Lesson closes, reread its Handoff and active Trace, then update Current
Position, Plan Summary, and the still-unactivated Lesson candidates. Do not
rewrite an activated Lesson, whether it is active, paused, or terminal. Do not
describe a Candidate as prepared.

Materialize only the approved next Lesson during preparation. Multiple
Candidates may remain, be reordered, or be removed; dependencies must refer to
real handles in the same Lesson Tree.

## Complete or replan

Before the first decision to complete a Plan, run one focused
counter-evidence review of the proposed conclusion, boundary, and decisive
sources. Present the conclusion, limitations, open questions, and next choice
to the student. Completion requires both direct evidence against the Plan
standard and the student's explicit choice.

When completed, write one source-grounded Plan Handoff to the Roadmap:
learner Claims, teaching Claims, open questions, and canonical lower-level
sources. If the sources support only an index, preserve a source-only Handoff
instead of inventing a conclusion. A new Plan starts only when a new learning
cycle is chosen; otherwise adapt the current Plan's unactivated branches.

Reread the Plan, its tree, Handoff, and Roadmap link before reporting the
persisted state.
