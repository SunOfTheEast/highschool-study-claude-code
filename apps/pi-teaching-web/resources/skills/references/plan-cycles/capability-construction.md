# Capability-construction cycle

## Use when

Use this when the student has not yet formed an important concept, representation,
method, or structural connection that the Roadmap requires.

## Stage problem

The student needs to move from prerequisites or fragments to an idea they can explain
and independently use on material different from the teaching example.

## Minimum starting information

Know the target capability, the direct test, the genuinely necessary prerequisites,
and enough about the student's current explanation or attempt to avoid reteaching
what is already available. A separate diagnostic Lesson is optional.

## Default phase graph

```text
prerequisites and baseline
→ construct one idea
→ guided near transfer
→ one-variable variation
→ unseen independent transfer
→ direct check
```

## Evidence sought at each phase

- Baseline: which prerequisites are independently retrievable.
- Construction: whether the student can explain the decisive relation.
- Near transfer: whether the idea survives a different task with fading support.
- Variation: whether a boundary or changed condition is understood.
- Independent transfer and check: whether the declared standard is genuinely used
  without help that supplies the route.

## Branches and loops

Repair a missing prerequisite briefly, then return. Skip repeated construction when
the student already explains and transfers. Use a contrast or counterexample when an
overgeneralisation appears. Repeat transfer with a genuinely different task when
support has changed what the first task can prove.

## Sufficient completion boundary

The direct test or an honest equivalent has been exercised, and the student can
explain and independently use the target within the Plan's stated boundary. One
taught example or one imitative success is insufficient.

## Common Lesson templates

Concept or method construction, deliberate practice, and capability assessment;
insert remediation only when an evidenced problem needs it.

## Common misjudgment

Do not equate “the teacher explained it” or “the student followed the prototype” with
a constructed, transferable capability.

A phase may occupy zero, one, or several Lessons and may be skipped, repeated,
merged, or revisited. Do not encode fixed Lesson IDs, counts, minutes, mastery scores,
or runtime phase fields.
