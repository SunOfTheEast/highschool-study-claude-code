# Concept or method construction Lesson

## Use when

Use this when the student needs to form a new concept, representation, method, or
structural connection rather than merely practise something already understood.

## Intended change

Move from isolated words or imitation to a usable idea the student can explain,
recognise in another setting, and begin independently.

## Necessary teaching functions

- Activate only the prerequisite knowledge needed now.
- Create a reason for the new idea, then model or co-construct one clear prototype.
- Require the student to explain the decisive relation in their own language.
- Separate the teaching example from later transfer and exit tasks.
- Surface a nearby boundary or contrast so the method does not become a slogan.

## Default activity path and depth

Use a prerequisite check, a purposeful situation or source, one taught prototype,
a different near-transfer task, a contrast or boundary, and an independent exit.
Usually one example is taught and 2–3 genuine tasks are answered by the student.
This is preparation capacity, not a quota.

## Adaptation from student evidence

Skip prerequisites the student retrieves fluently. Slow down at the first missing
reason, not at every algebraic detail. If transfer fails, compare the new task with
the prototype before adding more examples. Media is included only when a later
question or task uses what the student observes in it.

## First-pass information boundary

The Lesson may name its concept or method. Do not reveal the decisive reasoning of
an unseen transfer or exit task before the student has had a real attempt.

## Sufficient closing evidence

The student explains the central idea and independently makes meaningful progress
on a different task. This is local construction evidence; durable availability or
broad transfer belongs to later practice and assessment.

## Reflection default

Use a short explanation such as “what changed in how you saw the problem?” only when
it helps consolidate the new relation. Do not force a generic reflection paragraph.

Do not turn this default into fixed minutes, fixed Block IDs, runtime fields, or a
template-specific schema.
