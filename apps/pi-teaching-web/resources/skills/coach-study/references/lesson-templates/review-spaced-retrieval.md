# Spaced-retrieval review Lesson

## Use when

Use this when older learning must be checked after delay and outside its original
Lesson context. A future review queue may offer candidates, but Coach decides whether
any belong in this Lesson.

## Intended change

Discover what remains retrievable cold, restore weak access efficiently, and verify
the repair on new material without confusing prompted relearning with retention.

## Necessary teaching functions

- Start with mixed cold retrieval before recap or method cues.
- Sample distinct older ideas rather than replaying one recent Lesson.
- Separate independent retrieval from later teaching.
- Teach only observed weak points, then independently recheck them elsewhere.

## Default activity path and depth

Prepare roughly 5–8 short mixed probes for cold retrieval, followed by local teaching
and new rechecks only where needed. Probes may be definitions, explanations, route
choices, one decisive step, or short problems. The count is capacity, not a quota.

## Adaptation from student evidence

Skip teaching for items retrieved accurately with reasons. When a failure reflects
wording rather than knowledge, clarify without supplying the method. If the student
needs instruction, end that item's cold status and schedule a separate recheck.

## First-pass information boundary

Keep the first pass free of recap, method menus, route cues, and answers that would
prime retrieval. The review purpose, broad source period, and activity count may be
public.

## Sufficient closing evidence

Record which older ideas were independently available, which returned only after
support, and which still need work. Same-session repair is not proof of long-term
retention.

## Reflection default

Use a short retrieval strategy or confidence comparison only when it improves future
review. Do not require reflection after every probe.

Do not turn this default into fixed minutes, fixed Block IDs, runtime fields, or a
template-specific schema.
